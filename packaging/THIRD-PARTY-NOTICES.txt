Face Studio for EA Cricket 07 - third-party components
========================================================

Face Studio bundles the following components. Their licence texts are in the "licenses" folder.

OTools 0.179 (tools\otools)
  Converts Cricket 07 .o models to and from glTF.
  Copyright (c) 2020-2025 Dmitri. MIT License - licenses\OTools-LICENSE.txt

MediaPipe Face Mesh V2 landmark model (tools\facemesh\face_landmarks.onnx)
  Google MediaPipe face landmark network converted to ONNX (no retraining). Used only to pre-place the
  front-photo markers in "Likeness from photos".
  Weights (c) Google LLC. Apache License 2.0 - licenses\Apache-2.0.txt
  Source: https://huggingface.co/naklitechie/face-landmarks-onnx (see tools\facemesh\README.md)

ONNX Runtime 1.20.1 (Microsoft.ML.OnnxRuntime.dll, onnxruntime*.dll)
  Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation. MIT License - licenses\OnnxRuntime-LICENSE.txt
  Its own third-party notices: licenses\OnnxRuntime-ThirdPartyNotices.txt

Helix Toolkit for WPF 2.27.0 (HelixToolkit.Wpf.dll)
  Copyright (c) Helix Toolkit contributors. MIT License - licenses\HelixToolkit-LICENSE.txt

.NET 8 runtime and WPF (the remaining .dll files, included so no .NET install is needed)
  Copyright (c) .NET Foundation and Contributors. MIT License - licenses\DotNet-LICENSE.txt
  Its own third-party notices: licenses\DotNet-ThirdPartyNotices.txt

Game assets
  The "templates" folder holds stock head and hair models and textures from EA SPORTS Cricket 07, included
  as starting points for making faces. They are the property of Electronic Arts. Face Studio is a fan-made
  modding tool, not made, endorsed or supported by Electronic Arts or HB Studios. Use it with a copy of the
  game you own.
